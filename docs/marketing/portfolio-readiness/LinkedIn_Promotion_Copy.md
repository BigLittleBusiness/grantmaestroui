## Recommended publishing approach

| Week | Post | Audience / objective |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Company-page post | Broad awareness and assessment starts |
| Week 2 | Founder post | Credibility, conversation and profile traffic |
| Week 3 | Partner post | Third-party distribution and new audience reach |
| Week 4 | Short follow-up post using a different opening question | Retarget engaged audience organically or through paid promotion |

Keep each post focused on the operational problem and the immediate usefulness of the snapshot. Do not claim sector-wide benchmark results until GrantMaestro has enough consented, methodologically sound data to support that statement.
